# Sellteo — supplier audit (2026-11-26)

**Status:** CONDITIONAL  
**Type:** Multi-category wholesale platform / aggregator  
**Fit:** Selective Home & Living, Travel & Organization, Lifestyle

## Confirmed terms
- 100k+ products, 10 departments / ~250 categories. Focus only on home, office, kitchen, organization, travel and small lifestyle products.
- Dropshipping from one order; account, XML, program and panel free; no minimum and no subscription.
- Business account with NIP and wholesale-program application required; decision usually within 1 business day, up to 5.
- Standard wholesale discount 2%; individual discount may be negotiated after regular orders. Payment upfront.
- Two XML feeds: full catalogue and price/stock. Compatible with BaseLinker and other systems; native Shopify integration not confirmed. Refresh cadence described as periodic, not exact.
- Available goods paid by 10:00 are dispatched the same business day.
- Supplier shipping starts at PLN 11.99/package; InPost free on merchandise orders >= PLN 199; >30 kg bulky freight PLN 40.
- Own label: no carrier charge through supplier, PLN 1.99 packaging fee. Supplier rates are gross.
- Neutral carton, no price, invoice or Sellteo marketing materials. Own label supported.
- Tracking number sent to store by email, not directly to customer.

## Returns and risks
- B2B no right to return without cause.
- Defects/non-conformity must be reported within 7 days with photos; damage-in-transit requires a damage protocol.
- Consumer returns from store's customers require prior individual agreement with Sellteo; supplier does not automatically accept them.
- 2% standard discount may be insufficient for Giovetta's 30% contribution-margin target after supplier delivery and payment fees.
- Warehouse location, exact feed refresh, Shopify native integration, tracking sync, VAT-exempt buyer acceptance and EU transactions remain unconfirmed.
- Do not import the entire catalogue; shortlist SKU-by-SKU, verify stock and all costs.
